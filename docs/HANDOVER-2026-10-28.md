## Decisions carried forward

- Keep HALO frozen and other existing faces byte-identical when adding a new face.
- Retain literal colours: indexed theme colours caused REDLINE's white screen.
- Retain flat editor declarations, captioned icons and Condition selection: nested native selectors caused blank/unusable editors.
- Use live-clock motion for new work. Infinite constant-target loops previously stopped after face switches, and whole-second interpolation introduced a wake pause.
- Keep fixed clock/readouts and a provider-owned shortcut. The user removed Timer in 0.3.x; it returned in 1.0.0. The stale face-standard sentence saying it remained omitted was corrected during this takeover.
- The current phone app only installs. Historical measurement tools remain outside face APKs and unused; the older conversation's diagnostics plan does not restore them.
- Publication still needs explicit approval. This takeover prepares a reviewable local candidate and does not push or publish.

## Cyberdeck design reading

Read README, DESIGN, ARCHITECTURE, WORKING and project guidance alongside palettes, wallpaper source and existing screenshots. The consistent language is black space, uppercase instrument typography, crisp angular frames, restrained light and purposeful motion. Recent changes also remove visual noise: bokeh, an unwanted figure, horizon slabs and PRISM's bright tubes. NEXUS therefore gives priority to a clean clock aperture and places detail on the surrounding structure.

NEXUS's new artwork combines that language with HALO's two-tone instruments, REDLINE's mechanical carriers and CIPHER's code orbit. See its [design and acceptance document](../faces/nexus/README.md). Cyberdeck source, wallpaper engines and existing watch faces were not changed.
